PHOTOS GO HERE
==============

Name your pictures:

  photo-1.jpg
  photo-2.jpg
  photo-3.jpg
  photo-4.jpg
  photo-5.jpg
  photo-6.jpg

That is all you have to do - the scrapbook picks them up automatically.
Until a file exists, the site shows a pastel placeholder in its place,
so nothing ever looks broken.

Tips
----
* Portrait crops look best (the frames are 4:5).
* Around 800x1000 px is plenty. Keep each file under ~400 KB so the
  page stays fast.
* Want more or fewer photos, or different filenames? Edit the
  CONFIG.scrapbook list at the top of script.js.
* .png and .webp work too - just match the filename in CONFIG.
