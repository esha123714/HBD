Lyrics Timer

Turns plain lyrics into the timed format the birthday site reads. Paste the words, play the song, tap the spacebar as each line starts. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — this runs entirely in your browser.

Step 1 — the song

Pick the mp3 from assets/music/. Use the player to find where the singing starts, then pause and rewind a little before you begin.

Step 2 — the words

Step 3 — tap along

Line 0 / 0 Time 0:00

Tap space the instant each line begins. Got one wrong? Backspace undoes it and rewinds a little. Don't worry about being perfect — you can nudge the numbers by hand afterwards.

Step 4 — save it

Download gives you a file called lyrics.js. Put it in the same folder as index.html, next to script.js. That is the whole job — the site picks it up on its own and nothing else needs editing.

Timing more than one song? Download each, then paste the inner lines together into a single lyrics.js.
